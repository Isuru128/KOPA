THE PHILOSOPHY

More than coffee.

KOPA is a small coffee shop built around good coffee, good conversations, and everyday moments worth slowing down for.

We source specialty micro-lots directly from ethical smallholders, roasting in tiny batches to accentuate each origin's inherent sweetness and complexity. By combining artisanal preparation with our table reservation and pre-order system, we ensure that your coffee is pulled at the exact minute you arrive.
